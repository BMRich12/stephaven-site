Two walks, one shared town

Take a 13-second tour of a town you can build with a friend on iPhone.

The camera passes cottages and market stalls before settling on a group of villagers by the canal. The clip has music and on-screen text.

Read the on-screen text

“Two walks. One little town. Build together on your own schedules.”

“Your steps earn your points. Each of you chooses what to build.”

“Meet by the canal? StepHaven · Free on iPhone.”

Can we build together at different times?

Yes. You could lay a path at lunch and a friend could place a bench beside it that evening. You each spend points from your own walks. When the shared town syncs through iCloud, you can both see what the other person built.

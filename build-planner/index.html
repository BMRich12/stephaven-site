Lamplit bench

182 points

Lay four gravel tiles along a bend in the path. Set the bench beside it, tuck flowers at either end and leave a spot for the lantern. Come back at dusk to see it lit.

Gnome garden

1,250 points

Leave a gap in a ring of cobblestones for the entrance. Put the gnome between two flower beds and plant the cherry tree behind him so you can still see his hat.

Dog yard

4,106 points

Start with a short gravel walk and a bench for company. Dot clover on the grass beside it. Save the dog house for last and meet the dog who moves in.

These are regular shop prices in version 1.0.6. All three plans use pieces from the free game. Buy what you need in the Shop, then place it from Build. If you’re sharing a haven, pick who brings which pieces. Each builder spends their own points.

How it works

Your iPhone already counts your steps, and so does your Apple Watch. StepHaven reads them from Apple Health, so there's nothing to start or track. Go for a walk, open the app, and build.

1

Walk

Every step you take is a point to spend. A walk around the block buys a bench or a lantern.

2

Build

Place cottages, trees, flower beds, ponds and paths. An ordinary day on your feet pays for a house.

3

Watch it grow

Villagers move in, wander the lanes and head home after dark. Deer and foxes visit when there's enough green.

A look inside

Trains, boats and a town that glows

Lay a railway and watch a steam train pull into the station. Row a gondola down the canal or send a paddle steamer across the lake. When evening comes, every window lights up, and the seasons change through the year.

Fair by design

  • No ads and no waiting timers.
  • Steps can't be bought. Everything in your town was paid for by walking.
  • Your step count stays on your phone. It isn't sent to us or anyone else.
  • Works offline. A connection is only needed for iCloud backup and shared towns.

Questions

Is StepHaven free?

Yes, it's free to play, with one town of your own and one to share. StepHaven+ is an optional membership that adds more Workshop shapes and styles and as many towns as you like. Item packs such as Cottage Garden are sold separately. Steps themselves are never for sale.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone counts your steps on its own. A Watch adds the steps you take without your phone, and it's needed for Ember workouts and wheelchair pushes.

Can I play if I use a wheelchair?

Yes. Turn on Count Wheelchair Pushes and the pushes your Apple Watch records earn points just like steps.

What does StepHaven do with my health data?

It reads your steps (and pushes or Ember workout data, if you use them) from Apple Health on your phone, only to work out your points. That data never leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Start a shared town in the app and send a friend the code. You both build in the same place, while your steps, points and items stay your own.

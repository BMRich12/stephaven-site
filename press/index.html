About

StepHaven turns the steps you walk into a little town. Your iPhone already counts your steps; StepHaven reads them from Apple Health and turns each step into a point to spend on cottages, gardens, ponds, trees and villagers. A short walk buys a flower bed. A long weekend hike might buy a lighthouse.

Most step counters show you a number. StepHaven shows you a town that only exists because you went outside.

Features

  • Over 500 things to build, including more than 40 kinds of tree
  • A Workshop for designing your own houses, cottages and shops
  • Villagers who move in, wander, chat and visit each other
  • The real time of day and real seasons: dawn mist, lit windows at night, snow in winter
  • Shared havens: build one town with friends over iCloud
  • Wild animals that visit and leave presents
  • Wheelchair pushes count the same as steps
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Live Activity for workouts
  • Every building, tree and animal is drawn in code

At launch

  • Halloween Town, through October 31: haunted houses, jack-o'-lanterns and a candlelit fair.
  • The Pioneer Gnome: everyone who downloads StepHaven by November 8 gets a founders' statue that won't be offered again.

Privacy

Step data never leaves the phone. There are no accounts and no ads. Read the privacy policy.

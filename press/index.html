About

StepHaven turns the steps you walk into a little town. Your iPhone already counts your steps; StepHaven reads them from Apple Health and turns each step into a point to spend on cottages, gardens, ponds, trees and villagers. A short walk buys a flower bed. A long weekend hike might buy a lighthouse.

Most step counters show you a number. StepHaven shows you a town that only exists because you went outside.

Features

  • Over 500 things to build, including more than 40 kinds of tree
  • A Workshop for designing your own houses, cottages and shops
  • Villagers who move in, wander, chat and visit each other
  • The real time of day and real seasons: dawn mist, lit windows at night, foggy evenings, snow in winter
  • A travelling fair that arrives on every real full moon, worked out on the phone from the moon's actual cycle
  • Visiting merchants with things you can't buy anywhere else, and a daily present by the town flag
  • Shared havens: build one town with friends over iCloud
  • Wild animals that visit and leave presents
  • Wheelchair pushes count the same as steps
  • Ember: light it before a bike ride, swim or other workout, and the exercise earns points too
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Live Activity for workouts
  • Every building, tree and animal is drawn in code

For a limited time

  • The Pioneer Gnome: everyone who downloads StepHaven early gets a founders' statue that won't be offered again.

Privacy

Step data never leaves the phone. There are no accounts and no ads. Read the privacy policy.

Long, about 150 words

StepHaven turns your daily walk into a cozy little town. Your iPhone already counts your steps; StepHaven reads them from Apple Health and turns them into points you spend on cottages, flower beds, rivers, bridges, trains and more than 500 other things to build. A short stroll buys a garden; a long weekend hike might buy a lighthouse. There are no ads and points can never be bought. The town keeps real time: lamps come on at dusk, fog rolls in on some nights, snow falls in winter, and a travelling fair arrives on every real full moon. Villagers move in, wander and chat, wild animals visit, and a Workshop lets you design your own houses. Friends can share one town, each building with their own steps. Wheelchair pushes and workouts count too. Every building, tree and animal was drawn in code by one developer, and step data never leaves the phone.

Angles for a story

  • One developer, all of it drawn in code. The town's art is not image files: every cottage, tree and villager is drawn by the game's own code.
  • Walking is the only way to earn. No ads and no way to buy points. The paid parts (a membership and item packs) add variety, never steps.
  • Built for more than walkers. Wheelchair pushes count like steps, workouts can earn through Ember, and the game follows iOS accessibility settings.
  • Real time, real sky. Time of day, seasons, weather and the full-moon fair all follow the real clock and calendar, worked out on the phone.
  • Private by design. Step and workout data is read on the phone and never sent anywhere. No accounts.

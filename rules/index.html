Communityrules

Everyone agrees to these before building in a haven with friends or sharing a haven by link.

The rules

  • Be kind to the people you build with.
  • No hateful, sexual or violent content, in names or in what you build.
  • That includes symbols, words or shapes drawn with paint, paths, walls or anything else.
  • No names or builds meant to offend, harass or frighten anyone.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive players.

Names other players can see (player, haven and pet names) go through a filter, and names it catches are never shown to anyone else.

Shared havens

A shared haven is a town you build together with friends. Its creator approves each request to join, and can remove or block anyone in it.

Sharing a haven by link

You can also send a link or QR code that lets someone look round a copy of your haven, as it was when you made the link. They can walk round it, but can't change anything.

  • A link works for 30 days and for up to 50 people.
  • Anyone who opens your haven can report it. Reported havens are hidden and checked by a person.
  • Breaking these rules can lose you the ability to share havens, for a while or for good. Your membership, havens and items are never taken away.

Reporting, hiding and blocking

In a shared haven: open Havens and press and hold the haven.

  • Report a Member sends us an email naming that player and haven.
  • Hide a Member stops showing their name and what they build on your iPhone. Undo it in Settings › Shared Havens.
  • If you created the haven, Remove a Member or Block a Member takes them out, and a blocked player can't ask to join any of your havens again.

In a haven someone sent you a link to: tap Report or Block at the top of the screen. Once you block a player, no haven they share will open for you.

What happens to a report

We read every report within 24 hours and act on it. That can mean taking a haven's link or invite code down from our servers so no one else can open or join it, stopping a player from sharing havens, or adding words to the name filter.

Contact

To report something, or with any question about these rules, write to northlaneapps@gmail.com. You'll hear back from the person who makes StepHaven.

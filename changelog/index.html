StepHaven is out

StepHaven is now on the App Store for iPhone. It's a cozy town builder you play by walking: every step you take becomes a point, and you spend those points on a little town that grows as you go.

What's in it

  • Your steps build the town. Cottages, trees, flower beds, paths, ponds, bridges, boats and trains, all bought with steps you've walked. Steps can't be bought with money.
  • Every kind of moving counts. Wheelchair pushes count like steps, and Ember turns rides, swims and other workouts into points.
  • The Workshop. Design your own houses, shops and cottages, from the roof to the flower boxes.
  • Build with friends. Share a town with a few friends. Everyone builds in the same place with their own steps.
  • A town that lives. Villagers, pets, wild visitors, travelling merchants, seasons, weather and day and night, all on your real clock.
  • Presents and rewards. A daily present, levels that unlock new things, and rare finds to collect.
  • Item packs. Cottage Garden and Castle & Crown, each bought once and yours to keep.
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so you can see what you're saving for.

Thank you for walking with us. If something isn't working, or there's something you'd love to see, write to northlaneapps@gmail.com.

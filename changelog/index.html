What'sNew

Every StepHaven update, newest first: what's been added, what's changed and what's been fixed.

Version 1.0.6November 17, 2026

Railway crossings and a welcome back

Lay a path across a railway and watch villagers wait for the train. After time away, a card catches you up on town life. This update also adds twenty Game Center achievements and lets you arrange or hide Build tabs in Settings.

Version 1.0.5November 13, 2026

A gnome on your Home Screen

Add the Gnome Window widget to your Home Screen. Shop search now handles typos, and the tutorial’s shopping lesson has a fix for players who got stuck.

Version 1.0.4November 10, 2026

A reminder for the Moonfair

The Moonfair can send a reminder when it comes to town. Daily presents arrive in new wrappings, and the Sets tab in Build lets you select a whole pack with one tap.

Version 1.0.3November 9, 2026

Invite a friend into your haven

Invite friends directly into a shared town and display badges on your profile. Passport covers, new wild visitors and a Cat Tree for a pet cat also arrived.

Version 1.0.2November 3, 2026

Send someone a passport to your town

A passport link or QR code lets a friend explore a copy of your town. For building together, shared havens gained invite links and join requests on the map. Look by the town flag for a post box, where letters sometimes arrive with gifts.

Version 1.0.1October 29, 2026

Tell us what to build next

A small update two days after launch: a way to tell us what you think, better support for two accessibility settings, and a cooler-running map.

New

  • The Haven Survey. Five quick questions about what you enjoy, what gets in the way and what you'd love to see next. Find it in Settings → Help → Share Feedback, or on the card that turns up after your first week. Send it and a Suggestion Box arrives for your town.

Improved

  • Reduce Motion is now followed on every screen: panels and menus appear in place instead of sliding or zooming.
  • Differentiate Without Color. The item you've picked to build shows a tick, and tiles a drag couldn't take show a cross as well as a red outline.
  • Kinder to your battery. The map slows down while you're not touching it, so your phone stays cooler.

Fixed

  • The Workshop's last shapes and choices can be reached again on every phone.

Version 1.0October 27, 2026

StepHaven is out

StepHaven is now on the App Store for iPhone. It's a cozy town builder you play by walking: every step you take becomes a point, and you spend those points on a little town that grows as you go.

What's in it

  • Your steps build the town. Cottages, trees, flower beds, paths, ponds, bridges, boats and trains, all bought with steps you've walked. Steps can't be bought with money.
  • Every kind of moving counts. Wheelchair pushes count like steps, and Ember turns rides, swims and other workouts into points.
  • The Workshop. Design your own houses, shops and cottages, from the roof to the flower boxes.
  • Build with friends. Share a town with a few friends. Everyone builds in the same place with their own steps.
  • A town that lives. Villagers, pets, wild visitors, travelling merchants, seasons, weather and day and night, all on your real clock.
  • Presents and rewards. A daily present, levels that unlock new things, and rare finds to collect.
  • Item packs. Cottage Garden and Castle & Crown, each bought once and yours to keep.
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, so you can see what you're saving for.

Thank you for walking with us. If something isn't working, or there's something you'd love to see, write to northlaneapps@gmail.com.
